# What Happens After You Learn All This

You'll be able to do what you originally set out to do — investigate, run, and
understand any AI video model you encounter — but you'll also have crossed a
bigger threshold.

## Concretely, after finishing this folder

You'll be able to read a research paper (like Wan 2.1 or AnimateDiff) and
understand what every section is actually saying — not just the words, but why
the choices were made. You'll be able to load any HuggingFace model, trace why
it's slow or broken, benchmark its VRAM, and patch it. You'll be able to
fine-tune models on your own data, serve them as an API, and export them for
use in C# apps.

## The gap that remains

Scale and production experience. These files teach the concepts correctly, but
until you've debugged a training run that silently diverged at step 47,000,
watched a model overfit in real time, or fought a DataLoader memory leak at 3am
— the knowledge is theoretical. That part only comes from doing.

## The natural next step after this folder is a project

Pick one concrete thing:
- Fine-tune a small model on 500 of your own images
- Build a RAG system over a document set you care about
- Train an AnimateDiff LoRA on a visual style you want

Something that breaks, requires debugging, and forces you to look things up.
That project will cement everything in this folder faster than re-reading any file.
